Screvo(이하 “앱”)는 이름, 이메일, 기기 식별자, 위치, 사용 통계 등 어떠한 개인정보도 수집·저장·전송하지 않습니다. 앱에는 회원가입, 광고, 분석(애널리틱스), 추적 기능이 없으며 인터넷으로 데이터를 보내지 않습니다.

2. 앱이 사용하는 권한과 목적

권한용도
화면 및 시스템 오디오 녹음화면 확대·판서, 화면 캡처, 텍스트 인식(OCR), 화면 녹화 및 컴퓨터 소리 녹음

3. 데이터 처리와 보관

  • 캡처 이미지와 녹화 영상은 사용자가 지정한 폴더(기본값: 동영상·사진 폴더의 Screvo 폴더)와 클립보드에만 저장됩니다.
  • 텍스트 인식(OCR)은 Apple Vision 프레임워크를 사용해 기기 안에서 처리됩니다. 인식한 텍스트(.txt)와 인식한 영역의 이미지(.png)는 클립보드와 사용자가 지정한 폴더(기본값: 사진 폴더의 Screvo 폴더)에만 저장되며, 환경설정에서 파일 저장을 끌 수 있습니다.
  • 캡처 기록은 앱 전용 보관 공간(macOS 샌드박스 컨테이너)에만 저장되며, 앱의 “캡처 기록 → 모두 지우기”로 언제든 삭제할 수 있습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다.
  • 단축키·펜 색상 등 환경설정은 Mac의 앱 설정 영역에만 저장됩니다.

English

Summary: Screvo does not collect personal data. All processing, including screen, audio and text recognition, happens on your Mac. No data is sent to the developer or any third party.

1. Information we collect

Screvo (“the app”) does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email, device identifiers, location or usage statistics. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not send data over the internet.

2. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionPurpose
Screen & System Audio RecordingScreen zoom and drawing, screenshots, text recognition (OCR), screen recording and system audio capture
Microphone (optional)Recording your voice during screen recordings. You can turn it off in Settings. It is requested only when you first record with the microphone.
Notifications (optional)Local alerts when a capture or recording finishes
Folders you chooseSaving screenshots, text recognition (OCR) results and recordings

3. How data is processed and stored

  • Screenshots and recordings are saved only to the folders you choose (by default a Screvo folder in Movies/Pictures) and to the clipboard.
  • Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device with Apple’s Vision framework. The recognized text (.txt) and the image of the recognized area (.png) are saved only to the clipboard and to the folder you choose (by default a Screvo folder in Pictures). You can turn off saving these files in Settings.
  • Capture history is stored only in the app’s sandbox container on your Mac. You can delete it at any time with “Capture History → Clear All”; it is also removed when you delete the app.
  • Preferences such as shortcuts and pen color are stored locally on your Mac.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because no information is collected, nothing is shared with or processed by third parties.

5. Children’s privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

Change history: October 11, 2026 — Section 3 now describes saving text recognition (OCR) results as files. Screvo still collects no information.
